# Ownership

## Definition of Ownership in This Domain

Ownership means **business responsibility**. The owner of an entity is the actor who has the primary claim to it and is most affected by its state.

---

## WorkspaceSession

| Aspect | Owner | Rationale |
|--------|-------|-----------|
| Business ownership | Student | The session represents the student's time and work |
| Who can create (entry) | Faculty | Faculty scans to initiate the session |
| Who can set category | Student | Only the student knows what work they will do |
| Who can close (exit) | Faculty | Faculty scans to record normal exit |
| Who can perform manual exit | Faculty | Faculty performs manual exit as an exceptional action |
| Who can override | Admin | Admin can complete or correct sessions |
| Who can submit summary | Student | Only the student knows what work they did |
| Who can view own | Student | Personal attendance record |
| Who can view any | Faculty, Admin | Operational and administrative visibility |
| Who can archive | Admin | Administrative action |
| Who can modify after completion | No one | Completed and Archived sessions are immutable |
| Who can delete | No one | Sessions are never deleted |

## Category

| Aspect | Owner | Rationale |
|--------|-------|-----------|
| Business ownership | Admin | Categories are system configuration |
| Who can create | Admin | Defines available work types |
| Who can rename | Admin | Maintains category taxonomy |
| Who can archive | Admin | Retires categories from new use |
| Who can view | All actors | All actors need to see available categories |
| Who can delete | No one | Categories are archived, never deleted |

---

## Notification

| Aspect | Owner | Rationale |
|--------|-------|-----------|
| Business ownership | Student | The notification is about the student's session |
| Who can create | System | Automatically generated by business events |
| Who can mark as read | Student | Only the recipient can acknowledge receipt |
| Who can view | Student | Notifications are private to the recipient |
| Who can modify content | No one | Notifications are immutable after creation |

---

## ActivityLog

| Aspect | Owner | Rationale |
|--------|-------|-----------|
| Business ownership | System | The activity log is a system-wide audit resource |
| Who can create | System | Automatically recorded for state-changing events |
| Who can view | Admin | Audit trail is an administrative concern |
| Who can modify | No one | The log is immutable by design |
| Who can delete | No one | The log is immutable by design |

## Cross-Cutting Ownership Rules

1. **Self-service data access.** A Student can always view their own sessions, notifications, and statistics.
2. **Faculty visibility.** A Faculty member can view any Student's session history for operational purposes.
3. **Admin visibility.** An Admin can view all data across the system.
4. **No data deletion.** Business data is never deleted. Entities may be archived, suspended, or marked inactive, but records persist.
5. **Immutability of completed sessions.** Once a session is Completed or Archived, its data is fixed.
6. **Manual exit is logged.** Manual exit actions are always recorded in the Activity Log with the Faculty member's identity and business reason.
7. **Admin overrides are logged.** Admin override actions are always recorded in the Activity Log with the Admin's identity and reason.
